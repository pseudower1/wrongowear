Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

wrongowear ("the App") is a personal developer application that uses the TikTok API to upload video content on behalf of a TikTok account that explicitly authorizes access.

Information We Collect

  • TikTok account authorization tokens provided via TikTok’s OAuth process
  • Video files and metadata required to upload content

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to upload and publish videos to TikTok at the user’s request.

Data Storage

TikTok authorization tokens and video files are stored temporarily only. No TikTok user data is stored permanently. All temporary data is deleted automatically after the posting process is completed.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

User Control

Users may revoke the App’s access at any time through their TikTok account settings.

Third-Party Services

This App uses the TikTok API in accordance with TikTok’s Developer Terms and policies.
