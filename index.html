wrongowear

Automation tooling for publishing and managing digital content across social media platforms.

What it does

wrongowear provides programmatic workflows for uploading, scheduling, and managing media content using official platform APIs.

How it works

The system integrates with supported platforms when access is explicitly authorized, enabling automated content publishing through custom scripts and tooling.

Platform integrations

wrongowear may integrate with third-party platforms, including TikTok and other social networks, in accordance with their published API terms.
