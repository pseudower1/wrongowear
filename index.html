wrongowear

wrongowear is a personal software project focused on automating the publishing and management of digital content across social media platforms.

The application supports programmatic video uploads, scheduling, and content workflows using platform-provided APIs and custom scripts.

wrongowear may integrate with third-party platforms, including TikTok, when access is explicitly authorized by the account owner.

wrongowear is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any social media platform.
